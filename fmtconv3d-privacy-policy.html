Privacy Policy — FmtConv3D

Publisher: N3GIS (LucasEvan)
Product: FmtConv3D
Website: https://n3gis.github.io
Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how N3GIS / LucasEvan (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the FmtConv3D desktop software and related support services. By installing or using FmtConv3D, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Overview

FmtConv3D is a Windows desktop application for converting CAD, BIM, and 3D files on the user’s computer. Conversion is performed locally. We design the product so that day-to-day file conversion does not require uploading your model files to our servers.

2. Information we process

  • License activation data. A machine identifier (Machine-ID) generated on your device may be used solely to issue and validate a license file (.lic).
  • Support communications. If you contact us by email or other channels, we process the contact details and technical information you voluntarily provide (for example product version, Windows version, error messages, and Machine-ID for licensing issues).
  • Local settings. Preferences and related files may be stored under your Windows user profile (for example %APPDATA%\FmtConv3D\ and %LOCALAPPDATA%\FmtConv3D\). These files remain on your device unless you choose to send them to us for support.

3. Information we do not collect by default

  • FmtConv3D does not require an online user account to convert files.
  • We do not upload your CAD / BIM / 3D source files to our servers as part of normal conversion workflows.
  • We do not sell personal information.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • issue, activate, and validate licenses;
  • provide technical support and respond to inquiries;
  • improve product reliability and documentation;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Third-party services

Optional SVF browser preview pages generated by FmtConv3D may load Autodesk Forge Viewer resources from Autodesk online services when you open a preview in a web browser. That activity is subject to Autodesk’s terms and privacy practices. After purchase, an offline Forge library option may be available for environments without internet access.

If you access our website at https://n3gis.github.io, hosting and related infrastructure (for example GitHub Pages) may process standard web server or access logs according to those providers’ policies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only:

  • when required by law or legal process;
  • with service providers strictly necessary to operate email support or licensing, under appropriate confidentiality; or
  • with your consent.

7. Retention

License-related and support records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for licensing, support, legal, security, or accounting purposes, and then deleted or anonymized where practicable.

8. Data security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information we receive. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive unrelated personal data in support emails unless necessary.

9. Children’s privacy

FmtConv3D is intended for professional and business use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International users

If you contact us from outside our primary operating region, your support or licensing information may be processed in the region where we operate support and licensing. By contacting us or activating a license, you understand that such processing may occur.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are posted. Continued use of FmtConv3D after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.

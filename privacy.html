This Privacy Policy describes how 2D Drawing Converter (“the Software”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you install and use the desktop application on Microsoft Windows. The Software performs local file conversion and uses offline licensing.

1. Summary

  • Drawing and CAD files you convert are processed only on your computer.
  • We do not upload your drawings to our servers or to a cloud drafting service.
  • Licensing and trial limits are enforced offline on the local machine.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use advertising trackers in the Software.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the Software, the following may be stored locally on your device:

  • Machine identifier — a hardware-derived machine ID used to bind a license file to your computer.
  • License / trial status — such as installed license files, trial start time, remaining trial conversions, and related local records (for example under your Windows user profile / registry as applicable).
  • Application settings — preferences you configure in the UI (for example language, output folder, font directories, export options).
  • File paths you choose — paths to input drawings and output folders remain on your machine for conversion; we do not transmit them to us.

The Software does not require an online account to convert files. Conversion output is written to folders you select on your local (or network) storage.

3. How We Use Information

  • To convert drawings and write results to locations you choose.
  • To enforce trial limits and validate offline license files.
  • To remember your application preferences between sessions.
  • To provide support when you contact us (for example if you send a machine ID by email to obtain a license).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your drawings or local license data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not operate a telemetry / analytics backend that receives your CAD files.

If you purchase through Autodesk App Store or similar marketplaces, those platforms process purchase and account information under their own privacy policies. We receive only what is needed to fulfill your order (for example contact details you provide for licensing support).

If you email us for support or licensing, the information you include in that email (such as machine ID, proof of purchase, and contact address) is used solely to respond and issue licenses.

5. Third-Party Components

The Software may include third-party libraries required for CAD import/export and related functions. Those components run locally as part of the application. This Policy covers the Software’s own handling of information as described above.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Converted output files remain under your control in the folders you choose; uninstalling the Software does not automatically delete those files.
  • Local settings, trial records, and installed license files remain on your computer until you remove them or uninstall and clear the related local data.
  • Emails you send us for support are retained as needed to provide assistance and maintain licensing records.

7. Security

Because conversion and licensing run locally, protecting your device, user account, and output folders is important. We recommend keeping Windows updated and restricting access to machines that hold confidential drawings.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Software is intended for professional / engineering use and is not directed to children under 16.

9. International Users

The Software is a local Windows application. Any personal information you voluntarily send us by email is processed for support and licensing as described in this Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of the Software after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
